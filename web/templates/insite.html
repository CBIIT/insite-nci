Intramural NCI Shared Images and Tools Environment)

The Intramural NCI Shared Images and Tools Environment (INSITE) is an NCI-wide collaborative platform for accelerated imaging data sharing and analysis. From website, intramural researchers can share imaging datasets and analysis code across the research community, access cloud-based computational resources, and leverage platform interoperability for streamlined data transfer and secondary reuse.
